## Verified descriptions for accessible text

All eight local derivatives were opened and visually inspected on the retrieval date.

- **Coast:** Wind-shaped pale dunes and a broad sandy beach beneath large clouds at Hvide Sande, Denmark. Tiny distant walkers appear at the right edge; none is identified as a demo subject.
- **Table:** A fresh loaf in a wire basket on red patterned cloth, with glass jars on a dark wooden shelf behind it. The source identifies Arkansas, United States.
- **Sewing:** A black vintage Jones sewing machine with orange thread and yellow-and-grey chevron fabric. The source identifies Cambridge, United Kingdom. Visible trademarks are incidental and are not MyLifeBook branding.
- **Travel:** Snow-covered Swiss Alpine peaks seen from above through clouds. This is an atmospheric travel illustration, not a record of a fictional trip or a view from a train.
- **Garden:** Clusters of pink, pale green, and blue hydrangea flowers against dark leaves. The source identifies RHS Garden Wisley, United Kingdom; do not label this Ellen's garden.
- **Milo:** Close-up of dark green leaves with delicate pale pink and white veins. The source identifies RHS Garden Wisley, United Kingdom.
- **Noor:** A turquoise skateboard with pink flamingos and pale wheels suspended before a wooden fence. The source identifies Brisbane, Australia; do not label it Noor's own board.
- **Sam:** A golden loaf on a white cloth beside butter and a knife. The source identifies Arkansas, United States.
