I kept a video of the first time I managed the turn without stepping off. It is eleven seconds long. For the first six seconds, my friend is filming their own shoes.

The important five seconds are blurry, but you can hear me shouting. You can also hear a bus and somebody dropping a water bottle. It is not going to win anything for sound design.

I had been practising in the flat space behind the community hall. Nothing dramatic. Just rolling, turning, and trying to make my feet agree about where we were going.

People who are good at something can make it look inevitable. They turn and their body is already there. Mine was still holding a meeting.

That afternoon it finally worked. I did the turn, kept rolling, and immediately stepped off because I was so surprised.

My friend said, “Again.” I said, “Wait, did you get it?” They looked at the video and made a face that suggested the answer was technically yes.

We watched it sitting on the low wall, passing a packet of crisps between us. I tried again before we left. That one did not work.

I still have the clip. I like that you can hear the whole afternoon around the thing I was trying to do.
